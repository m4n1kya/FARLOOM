# Meridian Data Retention Policy

## Scope

The Meridian Policy applies to Aurora Dynamics and to all subsidiaries,
including Helios Labs. It covers every production system that stores
customer, telemetry, or employee data, whether hosted on company hardware or
with a cloud provider.

## Retention Schedule

Beacon API request logs are retained for 90 days and then purged. Pulse
Engine telemetry archives are retained for 18 months to support seasonal
trend analysis. Sentinel Dashboard audit trails are kept for 400 days.
Employee records are retained for seven years after the end of employment,
as required by applicable labor regulations.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to the Meridian Policy require written approval from Mira
Castellanos and a documented business justification. Approved exceptions are
recorded in the exception register within five business days and expire after
at most twelve months unless renewed.

## Deletion and Disposal

Expired data is purged by an automated quarterly job, and purge runs are
logged to the compliance archive. Decommissioned storage media are sanitized
with cryptographic erasure before leaving a company facility.

## Ownership and Review

Priya Raghavan owns the Meridian Policy and is responsible for keeping it
aligned with regulatory requirements. The policy is reviewed annually each
January, and material changes are announced to all staff before they take
effect.
