# Aurora Dynamics Employee Handbook

## About the Company

Aurora Dynamics builds industrial telemetry software for energy and logistics
operators. Mira Castellanos serves as chief executive officer and chairs the
quarterly planning council. In 2023 the company acquired Helios Labs, a
streaming-infrastructure startup that now operates as a wholly owned
subsidiary of Aurora Dynamics.

## Offices and Teams

Aurora Dynamics is headquartered at Cascade Campus in Portland, Oregon. The
EMEA region is served from Lisbon Hub, which hosts sales, support, and the
developer-relations group. Daniel Okafor leads Platform Engineering and is
based at Cascade Campus, where most of the infrastructure organization sits.

## Working Hours and Remote Work

Normal working hours are 9:00 to 17:30 local time, with core collaboration
hours from 10:00 to 15:00. Staff may work remotely up to three days per week
with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require sign-off from the
relevant vice president and a yearly in-person week at headquarters.

## Mentorship: the Atlas Program

The Atlas Program pairs every newly hired engineer with a senior mentor for
their first six months. Pairing sessions happen biweekly and follow a shared
curriculum covering architecture reviews, incident response, and career
planning. Sofia Marques coordinates the Atlas Program from Lisbon Hub and
publishes the pairing roster at the start of each quarter.

## Data Handling

All employee and customer data handled by Aurora Dynamics is governed by the
Meridian Policy, the company-wide data retention standard. Staff must not
keep customer records outside systems approved under the Meridian Policy.
Questions about classification or retention go to the compliance office led
by Priya Raghavan.

## Security Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents must be reported within 24 hours to the
incident-response rotation. Reports include what happened, the systems
involved, and the data classes potentially affected. Filing a report is never
a disciplinary matter; late escalation is treated far more seriously than a
false alarm.

## Expenses and Travel

Business travel uses the company booking tool, and the meal per diem is
75 USD per travel day. Expenses above 500 USD require pre-approval from a
budget owner. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the trip's end.

## Leave

Full-time staff receive 25 days of paid time off per year, in addition to
public holidays observed by their home office. Unused days up to a maximum of
five may be carried into the first quarter of the following year.
